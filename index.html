Methodology

why it is not "real-time updates", and why that is stronger
Update cadence
No source in the defence-market category genuinely updates every second, and neither Dealroom, Sixth Domain, nor Janes claim otherwise. Government budgets (SIPRI, EDA) update once a year, EU CORDIS updates monthly, and news or press sources update as events happen. The product should listen to each source exactly as often as it genuinely changes, and honestly show last verified for every number.
Primary sources
SIPRI Military Expenditure Database, European Defence Agency Defence Data, World Bank military-expenditure indicators (REST API), EU CORDIS open dataset / API (Horizon Europe, EDF-adjacent research).
Secondary sources
Trade press (Reuters, Breaking Defense, DeepTech Build), official company/investor press releases, government procurement announcements.
Confidence levels
verified - cross-checked against a primary or official source. sample - placeholder structure, not yet backed by a source.
AI layer rule
Database first. The assistant answers only from ingested, sourced records; it calculates from retrieved data and always cites the underlying rows. It never invents a funding amount, date or investor.
